This year, SubZero Robotics joined forces with the Duluth East Daredevils (Team 2512) to write for the BlueDevil Press. The BlueDevil Press covered both the Lake Superior and Northern Lights Regionals held at the DECC in Duluth, and student articles were also published in papers including the *Duluth News Tribune*.

Thank you for this opportunity, 2512!
