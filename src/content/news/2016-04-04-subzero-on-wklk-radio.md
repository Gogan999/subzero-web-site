SubZero had the opportunity to go live on WKLK 1230 AM and 96.5 FM to talk about our 2016 season and to promote our fundraising for the FIRST Championship. It was an excellent experience for us. Thank you to Dwight Cadwell at WKLK for inviting us onto his Coaches Corner morning show!

We also invited everyone to our chili feed fundraiser in the Esko High School cafeteria, with chili, desserts, activities for kids, and a chance to see our pit and slideshows from the season.
