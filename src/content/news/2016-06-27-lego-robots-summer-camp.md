SubZero is thrilled to announce that our LEGO robot summer camp is finally happening! The camp gives students a chance to learn what LEGO robots can do. Each group's objective is to program their robot just right to get over a series of obstacles.

Check out the [camp photo album](/gallery/2016-lego-camp/) to meet the teams and see the winning robots.
