After two years of cancelled and at-home seasons, SubZero was back at the DECC for the 2022 Lake Superior Regional, playing **RAPID REACT**.

The team finished qualifications **ranked 7th of 58** with a 6-3 record. As **captain of Alliance 5**, we fought through the playoff bracket to the **semifinals**, falling in a tiebreaker.

The judges recognized our robot's control system with the **Innovation in Control Award**, which celebrates an innovative control system or application of control components to provide unique machine functions.

Later in March we traveled to the **Colorado Regional**, going 8-5 in qualifications (13th of 41) and reaching the quarterfinals with Alliance 7. The season wrapped up at the MSHSL State Championship in May.
