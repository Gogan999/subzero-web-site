The 2023 game, **CHARGED UP**, challenged robots to pick up cones and cubes, score them on a three-level grid, and balance on the charge station. Our robot, **Aftermath**, was a 124-pound, six-wheel tank-drive robot that could pick up game pieces from the floor and substations and score on all three levels.

## Lake Superior Regional

Aftermath went **7-2 in qualifications**, finishing **7th of 60**. As **captain of Alliance 4**, we played deep into the double-elimination playoff bracket.

## Iowa Regional

At the Iowa Regional in Cedar Falls we went 6-4 in qualifications. The judges honored SubZero with the **Gracious Professionalism Award**, which recognizes a team that shows outstanding gracious professionalism, competing hard while helping and respecting everyone around them.
