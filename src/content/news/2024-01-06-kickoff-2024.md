On January 6, the team gathered for the reveal of the 2024 game, **CRESCENDO**. This year SubZero **co-hosted kickoff here in Esko with DNA Robotics** from Denfeld High School. **Ripsaw Robotics** from Cloquet and the **Hermantown Talons** joined us for conversations about the new game and team dynamics.

CRESCENDO's game piece is a foam ring called a *note*, which robots launch into the Speaker, place in the Amp, and score on the stage during the endgame. With the game revealed, we were officially in build season: six to eight weeks of designing, prototyping and testing our competition robot.

That robot became **Lil' Scoots**, our 10th-season robot: a compact swerve-drive bot short enough to drive under the stage.
