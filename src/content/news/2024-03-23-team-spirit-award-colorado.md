The 2024 season, **CRESCENDO**, was SubZero's 10th, and our robot **Lil' Scoots** made it a memorable one.

## Lake Superior Regional

After going 6-3 in qualifications, SubZero was picked for **Alliance 4**, which finished **third place overall** in the playoffs. After the first two weeks of competition we were ranked 18th of the 132 Minnesota teams that had competed so far.

## Colorado Regional

In March the team traveled to **Denver** for the Colorado Regional. We went 6-4 in qualifications, made the playoffs with Alliance 7, and the judges presented SubZero with the **Team Spirit Award**, which celebrates extraordinary enthusiasm and spirit through exceptional partnership and teamwork.

## Between events

Back home, the team kept improving the robot by adding Amp scoring and better shots from the podium, built LED signs for the stands, hosted an ARC practice field in Esko, and prepared for our Silent Auction & Dinner fundraiser.

## State Championship

In May, Lil' Scoots went **7-1** in qualifications at the **MSHSL State Championship**, finishing **8th of 36**.
