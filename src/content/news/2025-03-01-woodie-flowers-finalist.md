At the 2025 Northern Lights Regional in Duluth, longtime SubZero coach **Laura Zimny** was named a **Woodie Flowers Finalist**. The Woodie Flowers Finalist Award honors mentors who lead, inspire and empower students through excellent communication. It is one of the highest honors a mentor can receive in FIRST. The honor recognizes Coach Zimny's many seasons leading SubZero's safety, media and strategy work.

The team also earned the **Gracious Professionalism Award** at Northern Lights, and later in the season won the **Excellence in Engineering Award** at the Green Country Regional in Tulsa, Oklahoma, where we ranked 7th of 41 and captained Alliance 4.

Congratulations, Coach Zimny, and thank you!
