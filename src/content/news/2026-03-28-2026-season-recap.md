The 2026 FIRST Robotics Competition game, **REBUILT presented by Haas**, took us to two regionals this spring.

## Lake Superior Regional, Duluth (March 4–7)

At our home regional at the DECC, we finished qualifications **ranked 13th of 42** with a 6-5 record. We were picked for the playoffs by **Alliance 7**, and the judges recognized our robot with the **Creativity Award sponsored by Rockwell Automation**.

## Minnesota 10,000 Lakes Regional, Minneapolis (March 25–28)

At our second regional we went 5-4 in qualifications to **rank 14th of 51**, then played in the playoffs with **Alliance 8**, reaching the second round.

Thank you to our 2026 partners for making the season possible: Esko Public Schools, Irving Community Club, the Gene Haas Foundation, Mont du Lac, the Minnesota Power Foundation, Essentia Health and Cirrus Design Corporation.
