Welcome to the new home of SubZero Robotics, FIRST Robotics Competition Team 5690 from Esko, Minnesota!

Our old blog served us well from our rookie year in 2015, but it was time for an upgrade. Here's what you'll find on the new site:

- **[Season History](/history/)**: every season from RECYCLE RUSH to REBUILT, with events, results and awards.
- **[Gallery](/gallery/)**: more than ten years of photos, from building our first robot to the FIRST Championship.
- **[Outreach](/outreach/)**: how we share STEM with our community, from LEGO camps to parades.
- **[Sponsors](/sponsors/)**: the partners who make all of this possible, and how you can join them.

Want to get involved, support the team or just say hi? [Get in touch](/contact/).
